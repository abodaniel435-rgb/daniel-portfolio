# Problem Definition

With increasing tourism and demand for convenient booking, Hotel Miramar SG requires a robust online system so customers can view rooms and book them without phone or in-person friction.

**Problems addressed:**
- Difficulty finding suitable rooms at tourist destinations
- Manual booking processes
- Lack of real-time availability visibility
- No centralised management of rooms, customers, and bookings

**Solution:** A web-based Hotel Room Booking System with guest browsing, registered-user booking, and full admin control.
