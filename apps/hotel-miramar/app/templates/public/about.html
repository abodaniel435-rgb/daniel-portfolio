Our story

A refined urban retreat, built around the guest.

Hotel Miramar SG was conceived as a quiet counterpoint to the city outside: a place where the architecture steps back and the service comes forward. Warm timber, natural cloth, brass and stone do most of the talking.

From the curated art in the lobby to the linen on the beds, every detail has been chosen for how it feels at the end of a long day. The result is a hotel that is polished without being formal, and calm without being empty.

We are a small team, and we like it that way. It means the front desk remembers how you take your coffee, and the kitchen will cook something off-menu if you ask nicely.
