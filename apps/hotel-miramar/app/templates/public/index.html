Hotel Miramar SG is an address for people who like their luxury low-lit: dark timber, ivory linen, brass details and the bay moving quietly outside.

Set on Marina Boulevard, the hotel keeps the city close but the noise at arm's length. Rooms open onto harbour light, the rooftop pool faces the skyline, and the front desk knows your name by the second night.

Breakfast is served until eleven. Cars are parked for you. Nothing is rushed.
