    06 · Where these figures are weakest

    Three places your own number will beat ours

    The baseline is a literature mid-point

    It moves with season, variety, altitude, ridge position and who did the picking. Log three batches, weigh what you actually rejected, and put your own figure in the unimproved loss field.

    Transit temperature is guessed

    The calculator uses the temperature you type, which is usually the shade temperature you remember. Two weeks of a logger in a crate is a cold-chain audit, and it changes these tables for your farm.

    Aflatoxin is not arithmetic

    Hermetic storage suppresses mould by limiting moisture and oxygen. Whether your maize or groundnut is safe to sell is a laboratory result and nothing on these pages substitutes for it.

    07 · Check these against

    Who can confirm what these pages only estimate

    • Published post-harvest loss ranges for Nigerian perishables and stored grain - the source of every percentage in the crop table.
    • Your own weigh-in: the mass that leaves the farm against the mass the buyer accepts. Three batches is enough to replace a literature figure.
    • Two or three local quotations for each option in the storage table. A 20 - 30% spread between suppliers is normal at 2026 prices.
    • Your state agricultural development programme or a commodity association post-harvest officer, for the figures this tool can only estimate.
    • A laboratory test for aflatoxin in maize and groundnut before you claim the lot is safe to sell or eat.
    • A commercial cold-chain audit, or a data logger in one crate for a week, for the temperature the batch actually travelled at.
