    00 · Why keep a ledger

    The projection is a guess until you weigh it

    Every figure the calculator shows is drawn from published ranges for Nigerian crops. Your ridge, your variety and your rain year disagree with all of them, and only your own weighed loss can settle the argument.

    It is not a backup

    Batches live in this browser's storage, in the cropkeep:batches key. Clear site data or change phones and they are gone. Export the CSV before you do either.

    The difference is the whole point

    A positive difference means your handling was better than projected, and you can spend on it with confidence. A negative one means the baseline was too kind and the technology ranking is overstated.

    It is the sheet you hand over

    Printed, it carries crop, tonnage, value, the storage chosen, projected loss and weighed actual for every batch - what a cooperative store manager or a buyer actually asks for.

    It makes no safety claim

    Weight and value only. Aflatoxin and pesticide residue are laboratory findings, and nothing logged here is evidence that a lot is safe to sell or eat.

    02 · Turning the record into a decision

    What to do with these numbers

    A record is only useful if something changes because of it. This is the cadence the entries support.

    Before the cut

    Log the batch, do not describe it

    Weight, gate price, days to a buyer and the temperature where it will actually sit. A batch logged from memory afterwards is a story, not a record.

    On the day of sale

    Enter the weighed actual

    The mass or naira the buyer rejected. The difference column only fills when you do, and it is the column that makes the estimate better.

    Every three batches

    Replace the published baseline

    Open Limits in the calculator, put your own measured loss in the unimproved field, and stop projecting from the literature.

    Weekly

    Look at margin days, not loss

    Rows with a margin under half the trip are the ones that will be sold at the panic price next week. Cool them or shorten the journey.

    Season end

    Export the CSV and start fresh

    Twelve months of browser rows is not an archive. The export is what an aggregator, a store manager or a buyer can be handed.

    Next season

    Bring the record to the adviser

    Three weeks of weighed losses is a better brief than any estimate, including this one, when your state programme or a cold-chain audit comes to look.

    Next

    Log the next batch, or weigh the last one

    The ledger writes straight from the calculator - no account, no upload, no lead form.
