    02 · PAYE bands

    The bands are annual. Applied monthly.

    05 · Where these numbers can differ

    Three places a correct payslip still gets challenged

    Your state board

    PAYE is collected by the state where the employee works, and several boards publish their own forms, deadlines and rounding. The bands are federal; the filing is not.

    The package definition

    Basic, housing and transport are the statutory core. A 'other benefits' line can be argued in or out of the pension base — write your policy down and apply it the same way every month.

    Mid-year changes

    A raise in June means two different annual averages. Recompute the year to date instead of changing only the next slip, or the employee will be over- or under-deducted until March.

    06 · What to check these against

    Sources worth opening before you file

    • Personal Income Tax Act (as amended by the Finance Acts) — the band widths and the relief formula.
    • Pension Reform Act 2014 and PenCom guidelines — contribution rates, the 7-working-day rule, RSA transfer rules.
    • Nigeria Housing Fund Act 2022 — the 2.5% employee and employer contributions and the loan terms.
    • Your state's board of revenue — the PAYE return form, the remittance date and any state levy.
    • FIRS company income tax guidance — the ₦25m small-company threshold and the ITF position.
    • The National Minimum Wage Act 2024 — the floor every package in this tool must clear.
