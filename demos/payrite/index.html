04 · The maths, in the open

The rules this tool applies.

Nothing is hidden inside a black box. Change the bands if your state or your assessor uses a different table — the computation follows.

PAYE bands (per year, after reliefs)
Editable PAYE bands
BandNext ₦Rate

Rates are editable. Tax is applied progressively: each slice is taxed at its own rate, never the whole salary at the top rate.

Consolidated relief allowance

₦200,000 plus 20% of annual gross earnings, deducted before PAYE. It is the single most-missed line in a small payroll.

Rent relief

20% of the annual rent actually paid, capped at ₦6,250,000 of relief a year. Requires the tenancy evidence to be retained by the employer.

Pension and NHF

Employee pension is a percentage of gross (default 8%), employer adds 10%. NHF is 2.5% each side. Employee pension reduces taxable pay; employer contributions do not.

What this tool does not do

No VAT, no state-specific levies, no benefit-in-kind valuation, no withholding on dividends, and no filing. It is a computation aid — confirm the final figures with your tax adviser or FIRS guidance before paying anyone.
