Reference: the numbers behind the fare

The fare is arithmetic.
Habit is what stops
anybody doing it.

A driver asked why a seat costs ₦9,000 has one answer and it is usually 'that is the price'. These are the inputs RiderPool uses instead: distances, litres, wear, tolls, levies, the driver's cut, and the six riders who rode different parts of the same road. Every table here is editable in section 09 of the tool, and your numbers replace these the moment you type them.

    05 · The four split methods

    What each method collects, and how far it drifts from cost causation

    Cost causation is the yardstick the tool scores every method against: a rider's fair share is the amount the seats must bring in, multiplied by the kilometres that rider rode, divided by everybody's kilometres. On the worked example that is ₦89,922 across 1,064 rider-kilometres, or ₦84.51 a kilometre. Drift is the naira distance from that share, reported per rider, as an average and as the worst case.

    Method What it charges Collected Against ₦89,922 of cost Average drift Worst drift What it does to the short-distance rider
    Even split₦14,987 from every body, no regard for distance₦89,922₦0₦5,155₦8,479Collects the cost and then moves it: the 77 km rider pays ₦8,479 over his share and the two 245 km riders pay ₦5,719 under theirs. Fastest to explain, hardest to defend
    Distance share₦84.51 for each kilometre actually ridden₦89,922₦0₦0₦0Zero drift by construction, because it is the yardstick. It charges a full-length rider ₦20,706, which is more than the park has ever heard of, and it is the honest number
    Zone fareThe asking fare pro-rated by distance, rounded to the nearest ₦100₦39,100-₦50,822₦8,470₦11,706Proportional to a number nobody checked. Every rider drifts downward, so it feels generous while quietly being a discount schedule with a fairness label
    Park ruleFull fare past 60% of the route, half before it₦45,000-₦44,922₦7,487₦11,706Simple to say at the gate and short by half the trip. A convention of some parks, not a law and not a ruling, and the deficit is what later raises the fare for everybody

    Four methods because those are the four the tool carries, so this table is four rows by definition; the rider-by-rider figures are in the next one. The tool ranks them as average drift plus 35% of the worst drift plus a shortfall penalty per rider: distance share 0, even split 8,123, park rule 11,771, zone fare 12,779. Lower is fairer.

    11 · Where the argument goes wrong

    Three places a fare conversation dies, and what to put in its place

    None of these are disagreements about money. They are disagreements about what the number is measuring.

    The park rate is treated as the cost

    ₦9,000 is what people pay, ₦14,987 is what the seat costs to provide. Both are true and they are different numbers, which is why a full car of six still ends the day ₦35,922 short. Put both on the sheet and the conversation stops being about whether the driver is greedy.

    Everyone is assumed to ride the whole road

    An even split makes one assumption, that the money is shared, and on this board it is wrong: one rider covers 77 km of 245 and pays the same as two who cover all of it. Distance is not a courtesy, it is the only figure in the car that measures what each person consumed.

    The detour is priced as a favour

    Eleven kilometres off the line is ₦2,556 of fuel and wear and, on the tool's own pricing card, about 17 minutes of everybody's afternoon, or 24 minutes for all 25 diverted km at the ETA speed. A ₦198 charge to the person who asked for it recovers less than half. Either charge the real cost or refuse the stop.

    12 · Check these before you argue with them

    Where to verify a figure before you quote it to a stranger

    This page exists so you can see what the tool assumed, not so you can stop asking. The reference tables carry an as-of date and are estimates by construction.

    • Your own receipt book, or the fuel slips for the last ten trips. The kilometres per litre in these tables is an average and your engine has its own.
    • The park agent and the posted rate for that corridor on that day, for anything you intend to charge a stranger. That rate is market information, not a cost, and this page cannot tell you which one you are quoting.
    • The toll placard at the barrier and the levy schedule at the gate, both ends. Those are two different charges and both are commonly forgotten until the car is moving.
    • A garage that knows the class. What a set of tyres and a service costs per 10,000 km on a Sienna is the honest way to check ₦49 a kilometre.
    • Your own phone: the trip log and the fuel meter. Keep a corridor for three months and your own wear, levy and average speed replace the three weakest rows on this page.
    • The sample board in section 08 of the tool. Read it as fiction written into the source file: it shows what a fare ladder looks like, it does not tell you what a seat costs.
    • State and local rules on seating, permits and routing. They are not in this tool and the tool will not pretend to know them: what you may carry and charge is decided outside this page.
