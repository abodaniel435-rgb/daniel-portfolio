Questions

Frequently asked

Is my submitted data anonymous?

Yes. RentJury asks for the state, area, property type, rent and fees — never your name, phone number or street address. Reports are stored in your browser’s local storage on this device; nothing is uploaded while Firebase is unconfigured.

How is “fair” calculated?

Each area and property type has a reference band with a low, median and high annual rent. A quote within 8% of the median is stamped fair; 8–25% above is “slightly high”; above 25% is overpriced. The band describes a market, not a formal valuation of one flat.

Why is the agency fee checked separately?

Because a fair rent can still hide a stacked fee. Agency and legal fees are checked against a 10% ceiling each, and caution deposits above 50% of rent are flagged. The fee is usually the easiest part of a quote to renegotiate.

Can an agent get a report removed?

No individual can request removal of someone else’s report. The goal is an honest, crowdsourced reference, not a complaints channel against any one agent — so reports carry an area and property type, never an address or a name.

Is this legal advice?

No. RentJury is a pricing reference built from community reports and reference bands. For anything contractual, have the tenancy agreement reviewed properly before you sign or pay.
