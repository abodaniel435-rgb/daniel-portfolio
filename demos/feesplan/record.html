On this page

    00 · Why keep this record

    A fee plan is a promise with dates on it

    Each saved row is a figure you told a school you would pay, and a date you asked to be held to. Keeping them is what lets you prove it, and what shows you the month you over-promised.

    It is not a backup

    Saved in this browser on this device. Clear site data and it is gone. Export the CSV every term and keep it with the receipts.

    It is a working document

    Tick payments as the bursary issues receipts and the balance column becomes the figure you quote on the phone.

    It is your own evidence

    The stamped letter, the dated plan and the receipts together are how a disputed balance ends in one conversation.

    02 · Turning the record into a decision

    What to do with these numbers

    A record is only useful if something changes because of it. This is the cadence the entries support.

    Same week

    Tick a payment the day it is made

    The balance and the next date on this page are only true if the ticked lines are. Ask for the receipt, then tick the line.

    Confirm

    Confirm the date with the bursary

    The plan carries a confirm-by date for every payment. A phone call five days out is cheaper than an apology on the day.

    Admission

    Print the plan and take it in

    A dated plan the school has seen is worth far more than a promise made on resumption day. Print from this page and keep your copy.

    Term end

    Rebuild, do not edit

    Once every line is paid, build the next term's plan on the new figures instead of reusing the old one. The rise is not in the old file.

    New phone

    Export the CSV first

    This record lives in one browser. Clear site data and every saved plan is gone, so export and keep the file with the receipts.

    Next

    Add the next term, or clear what is finished
