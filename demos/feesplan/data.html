Reference · what a fee bill is made of

What a term really costs,
line by line.

A fee letter gives you one number and a date. The number is tuition plus eight extra lines, and the date is really three dates. Every figure FeesPlan applies is written out here so you can compare it with your own school's letter instead of arguing from memory.

    01 · The shape of the year

    Five numbers that decide whether you plan or panic

    The mismatch is simple: you are paid monthly and charged termly. These are the figures that describe the gap.

    ₦30,000what a ₦240,000 term becomes with eight months' notice
    435% a yearwhat a 15% monthly advance costs if you roll it every month
    ₦0the honest interest to assume on a sinking fund until a bank confirms a rate

    06 · Three figures parents are sold badly

    Where a fee conversation stops being arithmetic

    The bill is not the year

    A term figure multiplied by three is not what you will pay if the school raises fees for the session and charges the once-a-year items in September. Write the year first, then divide it.

    An advance rate is never monthly

    15% charged on the balance you still owe, for three months, is not 15%. The comparison section shows the cost of the month, the compounded year and the flat times twelve, because lenders quote whichever of the three flatters them.

    A fund that earns nothing is not a failure

    Target-savings and staff welfare accounts often pay very little, and a plan built on a rate nobody confirmed leaves you short in the month you need the money. Assume zero until your bank says otherwise in writing.

    07 · What to check these against

    What every figure above should be compared with

    • Your school's written fee schedule for the session, with the instalment policy attached to it.
    • The resumption date and the fee deadline on your own letter, which override every month named above.
    • The receipts for each payment and the statement of account the bursary issues at the end of a term.
    • The advance or loan offer sheet: the rate per month, the number of months, and what it costs to clear early.
    • The circle ledger, the contribution, the turn order and the payout date, in writing and visible to every member.
    • Section 09 of the calculator, where every band, assumption and rate used on this page is editable.
