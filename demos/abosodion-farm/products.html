Before you order

A few common questions

How do I actually place an order?

Tap any “Order” button or the WhatsApp icon — it opens a chat with us directly, with your item already written in the message. Tell us how many you want and we confirm availability and price.

Do prices ever change?

Yes, slightly. Feed and market costs shift, so always confirm the current price on WhatsApp before you finalise an order.

Can I get my broiler dressed (cleaned)?

Yes. Just mention it when you order — dressed birds are best ordered a day ahead.

Do you deliver?

We arrange delivery within Lagos depending on the size of your order. Pickup at Abomalu Farm, Ojo Barracks is also available. Ask us on WhatsApp for details.

How are the eggs packed?

Eggs are sold by the crate of 30 and graded big, medium and small before packing. Mixed crates are available on request.

Not sure what's in stock today?

One message is all it takes. We'll tell you what's available, confirm the price and arrange delivery or pickup.
