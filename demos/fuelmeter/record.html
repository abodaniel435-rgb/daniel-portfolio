    00 · Why this log is worth keeping

    A fuel log is the only witness you own

    Every row is four facts you measured at a pump: when, how many litres, how much money, and what the odometer said. Nothing else about your running cost survives an argument as well as that does.

    It is not a backup

    Stored in this browser under afrik:fuelmeter:log. Clear site data and the log is gone, so export the CSV every month and keep it with the receipts.

    It is the only number that describes your car

    The claimed km/L in the profile table belongs to a brochure and an open road. The pair of full-tank rows belongs to you, your traffic and your tyres.

    It is a working document, not an archive

    The fleet tab and the monthly budget are built from the same assumptions. When your litres per month drifts, go and re-enter the distance the vehicle actually does.

    02 · Turning the record into a decision

    What to do with these numbers

    A record is only useful if something changes because of it. This is the cadence the entries support.

    At the pump

    Enter the litres before you get into the car

    Litres is the field that becomes km/L and the one that is impossible to recover once the receipt is in your pocket. Money alone gives you a spending record and no measurement.

    Every fill

    Tick full tank, or do not

    The calculator learns economy only from consecutive full-tank rows of the same vehicle. A partial fill logged as full poisons the sample; one logged honestly just waits for its pair.

    Weekly

    Read the implied naira per litre

    That figure is your own price index. When it moves 5% you did not change your driving, the pump did, and the state row in the calculator needs updating to match.

    Monthly

    Compare implied economy to the claimed figure

    The km/L in the summary is all your rows pooled, so treat it as a floor, not a verdict. The learned panel on the calculator uses only proper full-tank pairs and compares them to your own baseline.

    Before you swap phones

    Export the CSV

    This log is one browser on one device, deliberately, with no account and no sync. The CSV is the only copy that survives clearing site data.

    Quarterly

    Print and read the sheet once

    A quarter of litres, money and odometer on paper shows the pattern the screen hides: which months the car worked, which months you paid it to sit.
