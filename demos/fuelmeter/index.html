00 · The hole in the arithmetic

Fuel is the biggest cost you can see, and the only one you count.

A go-slow burns petrol the engine is not using for movement. A worn tyre raises consumption without announcing itself. Pump prices differ across states and move without notice. Put those together and a trip that "feels" profitable quietly is not.

A · price gap

0/L

The same litre is not the same price

Petrol in Lagos and petrol in Kano are not the same number, and neither is stable. Every price here is an editable reference value you update from the pump you actually use.

B · idling

0L / hour

Go-slow burns fuel that moves nothing

An engine at idle drinks roughly 0.6 litres an hour on petrol. Two hours in traffic on a 60 km job is a real ₦1,956 that never appears on any fare slip.

C · the hidden tell

0

12% below your baseline is a warning

Log your odometer and litres and the site learns your real km/L. When learned economy falls more than 12% below your own baseline, it says the obvious: service the car, check the tyres.

      07 · Honest limits

      What this number is not.

      Consumption

      Driving style is a variable, not a constant

      Aggressive acceleration, under-inflated tyres, a lazy clutch, tinted windows and a boot full of stuff all move km/L by more than the table does. That is exactly why the fuel log exists: your learned figure beats any claimed one.

      Prices

      Pump prices move without notice

      The state table is a set of editable reference values with an "as of" date you set. There is no live feed here — static hosting cannot have one — so if the pump changed this morning, type the new number.

      Wear

      Wear costs are indicative averages

      Tyres, oil and servicing are amortised per kilometre from typical Nigerian prices. Your actual set of four, your actual mechanic, and that suspension work you keep postponing will differ. Depreciation is not included at all.

      Not a quote

      Nothing here is a quotation

      No fare, no contract, no repair estimate. Ride-hail, bus and minibus rates are arithmetic placeholders for negotiation, and a union levy or a park agent's demand is a conversation, not a formula.

      Storage

      One browser, one device

      Logs and fleets live in this browser's localStorage. Clearing site data clears them, so export the CSV before you change phones. There is no account and no sync — deliberately.

      Fuel type

      Petrol and diesel only

      Gas conversions, CNG and hybrid regen are not modelled. Diesel pickup, truck and bus figures use the diesel column of the price table; if you run CNG, edit the price row and treat the consumption claim with extra suspicion.

      • Trip totals exclude tolls, park levies, "orientation" charges and anything an agent demands at a barrier — add them to the load line or accept the under-estimate.
      • Cost per seat only divides the cost; it does not tell you whether a fare is legal, collectable or fair on your route.
