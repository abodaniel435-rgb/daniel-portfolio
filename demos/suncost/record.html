    00 · Why keep a ledger

    A solar purchase is a set of numbers you will need again in year four

    The bank you bought, the array you were promised and the crossover month you were quoted are the three things an installer will disagree about later. Write them down now.

    It is not a backup

    Saved on this device, in this browser. Export the CSV and keep it with the invoice.

    It is your negotiation record

    Three saved quotes make the fourth one comparable. Two make it a guess.

    It is the warranty file

    Chemistry, capacity and the date you saved it are what a battery claim asks for.

    02 · Turning the record into a decision

    What to do with these numbers

    A record is only useful if something changes because of it. This is the cadence the entries support.

    Same day

    Save every quote you size

    Change one assumption, save again. The ledger is how you show yourself which number actually moved.

    Before the meeting

    Print the comparison

    Installers negotiate differently with a written specification in front of them than with a verbal one.

    At negotiation

    Watch ₦ per usable kWh

    The average on this page is your market check. A quote 30% below it is a chemistry or capacity conversation.

    After install

    Keep the commissioning row

    Save the as-built system, not the as-sold one. That row is your baseline for the yearly capacity test.
