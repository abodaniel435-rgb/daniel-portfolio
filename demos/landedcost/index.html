01 · The problem

Three quotes, one shipment, three different profits.

The merchant who quotes from the Guangzhou invoice price has not yet bought anything. The one who quotes from the landed number has already decided whether this shipment is worth the container.

Guangzhou → Alaba

Phone accessories, 40 cartons

USD 8,000 of goods looks sellable at ₦1,520. Freight, duty at 10%, CISS, VAT on the duty-inclusive value and a 20% surcharge on duty push it far higher — and the rate on the day the bank actually funds the Form M is never the rate you used.

Istanbul → Balogun

Bags and shoes, garments band

Apparel sits around 20% duty with the ECOWAS levy and surcharge on top, plus terminal handling that is charged per container, not per carton. Twelve percent margin on the invoice is often four percent once the goods reach the shop.

Tin Can Island

Food, machinery, household goods

Perishables and spares have the worst enemy in common: time. Seven rent-free days, then a per-day rate that escalates by bracket. Nine days in the terminal can eat the whole margin on a low-value container.

02 · Shipment & Incoterm

What the supplier actually handed you.

The Incoterm is not decoration — it decides who pays freight and insurance, and therefore what the customs value is. Under FOB the freight and insurance you pay become the CIF assessable value. Under CIF the seller already paid them, so you must not add them twice. That single mistake inflates duty by 15–30%.

    Bands are ranges collapsed to a single indicative figure so the arithmetic stays checkable. A phone accessory at 5% and a finished handset on a different line are not the same assessment.

    Freight, demurrage and port charge defaults
    Editable charge defaults
    ChargeValue

    Sea LCL is quoted per CBM (₦28,000–45,000 indicative), FCL as a flat per-container rate, air by weight band. Demurrage brackets escalate by day.

    What the Incoterms change

    EXW — you pay everything from the factory door, so inland haulage and origin THC join the assessable value. FOB — the seller's job ends once the goods are on board; your freight and insurance are added to reach CIF. CIF — the seller's invoice already contains freight and insurance, so they are not added again. DDP — the seller delivers duty-paid: you get one price and no breakdown, so this tool shows the charges as an estimate of what is buried inside their figure.

    What this tool does not do

    No HS-code lookup, no valuation ruling, no attachment or ETLS relief claim, no WAPEX/duty-free entitlement, no forex application, no filing. It cannot know your terminal's current tariff, and it is not a substitute for a licensed agent's assessment. Use it to question the invoice, not to pay it.

      11 · Honest limits

      Where this number can still be wrong.

      Valuation is contested

      Customs can reject an invoice value it thinks is under-declared and substitute its own. Nothing here anticipates an attachment; a single revaluation can exceed every margin figure on this page.

      Terminal tariffs move

      THC, CTO and concession charges are revised by operators and shipping lines without much notice. Update the defaults in section 10 from the invoice you were actually handed.

      Rates are indicative

      The ₦1,520 default is a placeholder, not a market quote. The date you fund the Form M and the date the bank releases are two different rates, and both differ from the one you used to price the goods.

      No illegal shortcuts

      This tool does not model under-declaration, "all-in" clearing promises or the informal channel. Those are how consignments get impounded, and they are not a cost saving.

      Margin is not cash

      A 35% margin over 120 days of credit is roughly 9% a month of capital exposure. Check the cash conversion window in section 07 before you order the second container.

      Nothing leaves your browser

      Saved shipments stay in this device's storage. Export the CSV before you clear your site data or change phones.
