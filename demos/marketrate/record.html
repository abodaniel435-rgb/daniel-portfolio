MarketRate writes each trade to this browser as you log it, either from the trade form or from the haul calculator's Log this trip button. This page reads it back, sorts it, totals it and lets you export or print it, which is what makes it a record instead of a feeling.

On this page

    00 · Why keep this record

    A price history you are standing inside

    Every row is a number you actually paid or actually took, at a named market, on a date. Nobody can argue with it, including you, in six months.

    It is not a backup

    localStorage lives in one browser on one device, stored as marketrate:log inside it. Clear site data and the year is gone. Export the CSV every month and keep it with the receipts.

    It is the only honest price feed you have

    The reference figures on the data page are planning shapes with an as-of date. Your logged trades are what your market really did, and after a year they become the numbers you should be typing into the board.

    It is your evidence

    A cooperative splitting a season, a buyer disputing a price, a union arguing a levy schedule: all three want dated rows with the market named. That is what this table prints.

    02 · Turning the record into a decision

    What to do with the log

    A trade log is only worth the decisions it changes. This is the cadence that makes it pay for itself.

    Same day

    Log it before you forget the price

    An undated trade is not a record. Date, commodity, direction, quantity, unit, price per unit, market. The note field is for who paid the porters.

    Weekly

    Compare your average with the board

    The tool shows your average buy and average sell per commodity against the board's retail figure. If your average sell is under board retail, you are not selling dear, you are selling late.

    At the trip

    Remember what a logged margin is not

    Log this trip writes one buy and one sell, and the goods-only difference between them is not the trip profit. Transport, levies and spoilage stay in the haul calculator, where they belong.

    Monthly

    Export before you clear anything

    The CSV is the archive. This browser is not one, and a phone that gets wiped in the rainy season takes a season of your prices with it.

    At the union meeting

    Print the sheet

    Twelve months of your own dated buys and sells is the strongest thing anybody in the room can put on the table.

    Seasonally

    Check the glut against your own buys

    Look at which months your cheapest buys fall in. If they are not the glut months on the data page, that is the whole lesson and it cost you nothing to learn.

    Next

    Log another trade, or start the season again

    The trade form writes straight to this page, no account and no import. The haul calculator can write a buy and a sell for a whole trip in one press.
