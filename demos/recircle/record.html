On this page

    00 · Why keep this at all

    Three numbers, remembered in the same order by everyone

    Weight, price, record. A household that can state all three is treated differently at the gate, and a group that can produce them in writing can split money without a fight.

    It is not a backup

    The rows live in this browser on this device under recircle:ledger. Clear site data and they are gone. Export the CSV the day you sell, and keep it with the money.

    It is a working document

    Add every load straight from the counter, log scale checks from the weigh-in box, and the totals on this page become the statement you print.

    It is an argument, not evidence

    A dispute flag shows that your count and his scale disagreed by more than your tolerance. It does not prove intent. Weigh where you can see, or refuse the load.

    02 · Turning the record into a decision

    What to do with the ledger

    A record changes something, or it is just typing. This is the cadence these rows support.

    At the scale

    Write it before the money moves

    Date, material, his kilograms, your expected kilograms, the rate, the buyer's name, and whether you saw the reading. Sixty seconds at the gate, and the number is no longer only his.

    Same day

    Export the CSV

    localStorage is not a backup. Clearing site data or switching phones erases every row, and a school ledger is worth more than a household's.

    Weekly

    Read the flagged rows back

    One flag is a bad day. Three flags against the same buyer in one month is a pattern, and the pattern is the argument.

    Monthly

    Compare the blended rate

    Total naira over total kilograms is your real price. If it fell while your sorting got better, someone is repricing your grades and you have not noticed.

    At the meeting

    Print the statement, not the memory

    For a school, church or association, the printed rows plus the share-out sheet are what end the debate. Nobody argues with a signed column of kilograms.

    Each term

    Start a clean ledger, keep the file

    Two years of rows in one browser is not an archive. Export, name the file with the cycle, and clear it so the next term's totals mean something.
