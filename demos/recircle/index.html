01 · The leak nobody prices

It is not that the material is worthless. It is that nobody prices it in front of you.

A collector's arbitrary price, a scale behind the lorry, and no record afterwards. Five people use the same situation and none of them can answer the same three questions: what is it worth, was it weighed honestly, and is the load even worth the trip.

01

Estate resident, Lekki

Two bins go out monthly: one paid to the refuse man, one taken free by whoever comes for scrap. Sorting takes twenty minutes a week and has never been priced.

02

“Miles” scrap dealer, Ibadan

Buys everything at one price because he cannot sort fast either. His margin is the weight he gains on a scale the seller cannot check — and the risk of a wet batch.

03

School facility manager, Abuja

Carton and plastic from a term of lessons leaves with no weighbridge ticket, no receipt, no name. The custodian cannot tell the BOM what it was worth.

A carton sheet is about 1 kg per m²; the small box default is 180 g. If your jerrycans are dented and wet, weigh them and use “Actual kg”.

How every figure on this page is computed
  • Counted weight = quantity × editable weight per piece. An “Actual kg” entry replaces it for that line.
  • Sorted value = Σ (kg × that material's price) plus per-piece items at their per-unit price.
  • Mixed value = the same kilograms priced at the single mixed rate you are offered.
  • Wet / oily value = sorted value less each material's contamination penalty.
  • Trip cost = one-way km × ₦/km. Minimum viable load = trip cost ÷ the price offered per kg.
  • Dispute flag = recorded kg more than the tolerance below the kg your own item count implies.

No VAT, no levies, no gate fees, no aggregator margin — and no live market feed, because this page is offline.

What this tool does not do

It does not book a collector, move money, verify a scale you cannot see, or prove a buyer's intent. It prices, compares and records — you still have to be standing at the scale.

    10 · The honest limits

    What you should not trust this page with.

    Prices are a snapshot, not a market

    Buyer prices move with fuel, the naira, and what the aggregator in Apapa is short of this month. The “as of” date is the only defence. Edit it whenever you get a quote.

    A counted weight is an estimate

    Item weights are averages. A wet sack of sachets or a crushed bottle breaks them. That is exactly why the counter lets you override with your own scale, and why the ledger compares the two.

    No pickup has been booked

    The scheduler writes a record you can copy or print. It contacts nobody. Confirm the day, the price and the weight with the collector directly, in person.

    Environmental figures are ranges

    Only your kilograms are measured. Virgin-material displacement and CO₂e are published ranges with wide error bars, and the CO₂e number stays hidden until you ask for it.

    Records live in one browser

    localStorage is not a backup. Clearing site data erases the ledger, so export the CSV the same day you sell — especially for a school or association.

    Under-weighing needs a witness

    A flag in this ledger is an argument, not evidence. Weigh on a visible scale, or refuse the load: the tool gives you the number, not the leverage.
