What a kilogram is worth,
and what it is worth after he weighs it.

Nobody is cheating with a secret price list. The gap is that you do not know the rate, the deduction or the break-even weight, and he does. These are the four tables ReCircle runs on, dated and editable.

    Per-piece lines - phones, laptop boards, tyres - carry no percentage. They are simply repriced or refused, which is why the counter shows them separately. No receipt, no record: a deduction you cannot name is a deduction you cannot argue with.

    05 · Is it worth the trip?

    The minimum naira a pickup has to return

    Trip cost is one-way kilometres times the vehicle's rate, exactly as the scheduler computes it: push cart ₦40/km, keke ₦120/km, 3-ton truck ₦320/km. Divide that by the price you are being offered and you get the smallest load worth a phone call. The last column is the tool's own test for a comfortable trip: value at least 25% above the cost.

    06 · Where these numbers go wrong

    Three honest limits on this reference

    Your street is not my table

    Prices move with fuel, the naira, and what the aggregator in Apapa is short of this month. The as-of date is your only defence. Open the price table in the tool, type your buyer's number, and use his rate from that day.

    A grade you did not present is a grade you did not get

    Every sorted price above assumes the buyer sees a separated, dry, tied pile. Bring the same material in one sack and you are being quoted the mixed column, honestly, by his standards.

    Per-piece lines are the softest numbers here

    Phones, laptop boards, tyres and oil depend on condition more than weight. Treat them as starting points for a negotiation, never as a valuation.

    07 · Check these against

    Five things worth doing before you sell

    • Your own buyer's quoted rate, today, said out loud and then written into the tool's editable price table.
    • Two more depots on the same line. Same grade, different price, is a margin conversation; different grade, same price, is a warning.
    • A sealed 1 litre bottle of water on his scale, before your material goes on it.
    • The collector's real trip cost: the kilometres you both know, times the vehicle he is arriving in.
    • The tool's default table, restoreable in one click, so a bad week of quotes does not permanently bend your expectations.
