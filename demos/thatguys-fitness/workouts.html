🏋️ Back — Session A
01Superman Hold
4 × 12 reps▼
Play
Superman HoldPull the elbows back, pause, then return with control.

How to do it: Lie on your tummy on the floor. Stretch your arms out in front of you, like Superman flying. Lift your arms, chest, and legs off the floor at the same time. Hold it for 2 seconds. Lower back down slowly. Do it again.

💡 Tip

Squeeze your back muscles tight when you lift up. Keep your head looking down, not up.

02Reverse Snow Angel
3 × 15 reps▼
Play
Reverse Snow AngelPull the elbows back, pause, then return with control.

How to do it: Lie on your tummy. Put your arms by your sides, palms down. Lift your chest a little off the floor. Sweep your arms out and up over your head, like making a snow angel. Bring them back down. That's one rep.

💡 Tip

Make your arms go in a big circle. Keep your chest up the whole time.

03Table Row (Inverted Row)
4 × 10 reps▼
Play
Table Row (Inverted Row)Pull the elbows back, pause, then return with control.

How to do it: Lie under a strong table. Grab the edge with both hands. Keep your body straight like a board — only your heels touch the floor. Pull your chest up to the table. Squeeze your shoulder blades together. Lower back down slowly.

💡 Tip

This is like doing a pull-up sideways. If it's too easy, put your feet up on a chair.

⚡ Triceps — Session A
01Diamond Push-Up
4 × 12 reps▼
Play
Diamond Push-UpLower your chest with control, press back to a strong lockout.

How to do it: Get in push-up position. Put your thumbs and pointer fingers together to make a diamond shape under your chest. Keep your elbows close to your body. Lower your chest down to your hands. Push back up.

💡 Tip

This makes your arms work extra hard. If it's too hard, do it on your knees.

02Chair Dips
4 × 15 reps▼
Play
Chair DipsBend the elbows, keep shoulders down, press back up.

How to do it: Sit on the edge of a sturdy chair. Put your hands next to your hips, holding the edge. Slide your bottom off the chair. Bend your elbows and lower your body down. Push back up until your arms are straight.

💡 Tip

Keep your shoulders down, away from your ears. Bend your knees to make it easier.

🎯 Abs — Session A
01Plank
4 × 45 sec▼
Play
PlankBrace your abs and move slowly without swinging.

How to do it: Lie on your tummy, then prop yourself up on your forearms and toes. Your body should make one straight line from your head to your feet. Squeeze your stomach and your bottom. Hold still and breathe.

💡 Tip

Don't let your hips droop down. If it's hard, drop to your knees.

02Crunches
3 × 20 reps▼
Play
CrunchesBrace your abs and move slowly without swinging.

How to do it: Lie on your back with your knees bent. Put your hands gently behind your ears. Curl your shoulders up off the floor. Hold for 1 second. Lower back down slowly.

💡 Tip

Breathe out hard when you curl up. Don't pull on your neck with your hands.

03Leg Raises
3 × 15 reps▼
Play
Leg RaisesBrace your abs and move slowly without swinging.

How to do it: Lie flat on your back. Put your hands under your bottom. Keep your legs straight and lift them up to point at the ceiling. Lower them back down slowly, stopping just before they touch the floor.

💡 Tip

Keep your lower back flat on the floor. Going slower makes it harder.

🦾 Biceps — Session A
01Towel Curl (Door Curl)
4 × 10 reps▼
Play
Towel Curl (Door Curl)Curl with control, squeeze at the top, lower slowly.

How to do it: Loop a towel around a door handle. Hold one end in each hand. Lean back with your arms straight, feet against the bottom of the door. Bend your elbows to pull yourself up, like doing a curl. Lower back down slowly.

💡 Tip

The more you lean back, the harder it gets. Keep your elbows still — only your forearms move.

02Backpack Curl
3 × 12 reps▼
Play
Backpack CurlCurl with control, squeeze at the top, lower slowly.

How to do it: Put some books or water bottles in a backpack. Hold the backpack by its handle. Stand up straight with your upper arms close to your sides. Curl the backpack up towards your shoulders. Squeeze, then lower slowly.

💡 Tip

Add more weight to the backpack as it gets easy.

💪 Shoulders — Session A
01Pike Push-Up
4 × 12 reps▼
Play
Pike Push-UpPress or raise smoothly while keeping the core tight.

How to do it: Get into an upside-down V shape, like a triangle, with your hands on the floor and your hips up high. Bend your elbows and lower the top of your head towards the floor. Push back up.

💡 Tip

The more your body points straight up and down, the more it works your shoulders.

02Lateral Raise (Backpack or Bottles)
3 × 15 reps▼
Play
Lateral Raise (Backpack or Bottles)Press or raise smoothly while keeping the core tight.

How to do it: Hold a water bottle or small bag in each hand, arms down by your sides. With a little bend in your elbows, lift both arms out to the sides until they're at shoulder height, like the letter T. Hold for 1 second. Lower slowly.

💡 Tip

Lead with your elbows, not your hands. Don't shrug your shoulders up.

03Wall Handstand Hold
3 × 20 sec hold▼
Play
Wall Handstand HoldPress or raise smoothly while keeping the core tight.

How to do it: Face a wall. Put your hands on the floor near the wall. Walk your feet up the wall until your body is standing straight up, upside down. Hold still, arms straight, stomach tight. Walk back down carefully.

💡 Tip

Not ready for this yet? Practice holding the pike position on the floor first.

🫁 Chest — Session A (Upper Focus)
01Decline Push-Up
4 × 15 reps▼
Play
Decline Push-UpLower your chest with control, press back to a strong lockout.

How to do it: Put your feet up on a chair or bed. Put your hands on the floor. Do a push-up like normal — lower your chest down and push back up.

💡 Tip

Higher feet make it harder and work your upper chest more.

02Standard Push-Up
3 × 20 reps▼
Play
Standard Push-UpLower your chest with control, press back to a strong lockout.

How to do it: Put your hands on the floor, a little wider than your shoulders. Keep your body in a straight line. Lower your chest close to the floor. Push back up hard.

💡 Tip

Squeeze your chest muscles at the top of every push-up.

03Wide Push-Up
3 × 12 reps▼
Play
Wide Push-UpLower your chest with control, press back to a strong lockout.

How to do it: Do a push-up, but put your hands extra wide — wider than your shoulders. Lower your chest between your hands. Push back up.

💡 Tip

You'll feel a big stretch in your chest at the bottom. That's normal.

💪 Shoulders — Session B
01Elevated Pike Push-Up
4 × 10 reps▼
Play
Elevated Pike Push-UpPress or raise smoothly while keeping the core tight.

How to do it: Put your feet up on a chair. Get into the upside-down V pike position with your hands on the floor. Lower your head towards the floor. Push back up.

💡 Tip

This is harder than the regular pike push-up because your feet are higher.

02Front Raise (Water Bottles)
4 × 12 reps▼
Play
Front Raise (Water Bottles)Press or raise smoothly while keeping the core tight.

How to do it: Hold a water bottle in each hand at your legs. With a slight bend in your elbows, lift both arms straight out in front of you up to shoulder height. Hold for 1 second. Lower slowly.

💡 Tip

Don't swing your body to help lift — only your arms should move.

03Wall Handstand Push-Up (Partial)
3 × 6 reps▼
Play
Wall Handstand Push-Up (Partial)Press or raise smoothly while keeping the core tight.

How to do it: Get into a full wall handstand, upside down against the wall. Bend your elbows just a little to lower your head a few inches. Push back up to straight arms.

💡 Tip

If this is too hard, just hold the handstand still instead.

⚡ Triceps — Session B
01Tricep Push-Up (Elbows In)
4 × 12 reps▼
Play
Tricep Push-Up (Elbows In)Lower your chest with control, press back to a strong lockout.

How to do it: Get in push-up position with your hands under your shoulders. Keep your elbows close to your ribs the whole time. Lower your chest down. Push back up using your arms, not your chest.

💡 Tip

If your elbows stick out to the sides, it turns into a chest exercise instead.

02Chair Dips — Slow Tempo
3 × 12 reps▼
Play
Chair Dips — Slow TempoBend the elbows, keep shoulders down, press back up.

How to do it: Do a chair dip like before, but lower yourself down super slowly — count to 4 in your head. Push back up quickly.

💡 Tip

Going slow on the way down makes your muscles work way harder.

03Diamond Push-Up — To Failure
3 × max reps▼
Play
Diamond Push-Up — To FailureLower your chest with control, press back to a strong lockout.

How to do it: Get into diamond push-up position. Do as many good push-ups as you can, until you can't do one more with good form.

💡 Tip

Stop when your push-ups start to look sloppy, not before.

🎯 Abs — Session B
01Bicycle Crunches
3 × 20 each side▼
Play
Bicycle CrunchesBrace your abs and move slowly without swinging.

How to do it: Lie on your back with your hands behind your head and legs lifted. Bring your right elbow towards your left knee while you straighten your right leg. Switch sides, like you're pedaling a bike.

💡 Tip

Twist your body, don't just move your elbow.

02Reverse Crunch
3 × 15 reps▼
Play
Reverse CrunchBrace your abs and move slowly without swinging.

How to do it: Lie on your back with your knees bent up at 90 degrees. Use your lower belly muscles to curl your hips up off the floor. Lower back down slowly.

💡 Tip

The movement is small. Don't swing your legs.

03Russian Twists
3 × 20 reps▼
Play
Russian TwistsBrace your abs and move slowly without swinging.

How to do it: Sit on the floor with your knees bent, leaning back a little. Clasp your hands together. Twist your body to touch the floor on one side, then the other side.

💡 Tip

Twist from your waist, not just your arms.

04Hollow Body Hold
3 × 30 sec▼
Play
Hollow Body HoldBrace your abs and move slowly without swinging.

How to do it: Lie on your back. Press your lower back flat into the floor. Lift your shoulders and legs off the floor at the same time, arms stretched overhead. Hold still, like a banana shape.

💡 Tip

Too hard? Bend your knees in towards your chest instead of keeping legs straight.

Warm-Up Cardio — 10 minsJump Squats × 10 · High Knees × 2 min · Mountain Climbers × 2 min · Shadow Jump Rope × 3 min · Bodyweight Lunges × 10 each leg
🏠 Home Leg Day — Zero Equipment, Maximum Output Without a barbell, the goal is volume, tempo and single-leg work. Bodyweight leg exercises done correctly — with slow eccentrics, full range of motion and high reps — build serious muscle. Bulgarian split squats alone are one of the most effective leg exercises that exist, with or without weight.
🦵 Legs — Bodyweight
01Bulgarian Split Squat
4 × 12 each leg▼
Play
Bulgarian Split SquatLower under control, drive through the working leg.

How to do it: Stand about two big steps in front of a chair. Put one foot behind you, resting on the chair. Bend your front knee to lower your body down. Push back up through your front foot.

💡 Tip

Keep your body tall and straight. Do all your reps on one leg, then switch.

02Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift
4 × 10 each leg▼
Play
Single-Leg Romanian DeadliftLower under control, drive through the working leg.

How to do it: Stand on one leg with a little bend in your knee. Bend forward at your hips, letting your other leg stretch out behind you like an airplane. Stand back up straight.

💡 Tip

Keep your back flat, not curved. Look at a spot on the floor to help you balance.

03Jump Squat — Finisher
3 × 15 reps▼
Play
Jump Squat — FinisherLower under control, drive through the working leg.

How to do it: Stand with your feet apart. Bend your knees like you're sitting in a chair. Jump straight up as high as you can. Land softly and go straight into the next squat.

💡 Tip

Land quietly, like a cat, to protect your knees.

04Standing Calf Raise
4 × 25 reps▼
Play
Standing Calf RaiseLower under control, drive through the working leg.

How to do it: Stand with your feet apart. Rise up onto your tiptoes as high as you can. Hold for 1-2 seconds. Lower back down slowly.

💡 Tip

Do one foot at a time if you want it extra hard.
