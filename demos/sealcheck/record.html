On this page

    00 · Why this log exists

    A screening record is only worth what it lets you say later

    Every saved row is a pack you looked at, an answer you gave honestly, and a batch number that no other document in the shop will carry. Kept together, the rows become evidence rather than a complaint.

    It is not a verification record

    A score is a weighted summary of what you could see. Nothing in this table proves a product is genuine or falsified, and no row here should be quoted as either.

    It is a working file

    Save the doubtful packs, remove the rows you mistyped, and let the totals tell you whether you are actually inspecting or just guessing. Unanswered checks are excluded, so coverage matters more than score.

    It is your own evidence

    Outlet and town, batch as printed, expiry as printed and the reasons the tool recorded: hand this to the pharmacist at the premises and to your state drug officer with the physical pack.

    02 · Turning the record into a decision

    What to do with these numbers

    A record is only useful if something changes because of it. This is the cadence the entries support.

    At the counter

    Save before you leave the shop

    The line that matters is the one you can still fill in: outlet, town, batch as printed. Write the batch on the receipt and save the row, and you have something a pharmacist can act on.

    On delivery

    One row per consignment carton

    A pharmacy signing for stock should run the seam, foil and batch checks on a sampled carton from each delivery and save the row. Two months of rows shows which distributor keeps failing.

    Before reporting

    Print the row, not a description

    The printed report plus the physical pack, the carton faces, the leaflet and the receipt is what a regulator can do something with. The saved row is your index into all of it.

    Monthly

    Sort by score and read the bottom

    The packs under 66 and the rows carrying flags are your pattern evidence. A single bad pack is an accident; four from one outlet is a report to the state drug officer.

    Before you clear browser data

    Export the CSV first

    This log is not an archive. Clear the site data and every row is gone, and the batch numbers in it are the part nobody else recorded.

    Quarterly

    Compare coverage, not scores

    If mean coverage has fallen, you are answering fewer checks and congratulating yourself on higher scores. That is the one trend in this table that means you are getting worse.
