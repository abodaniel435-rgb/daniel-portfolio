How ordering works

Four messages, start to doorstep.

There is no checkout, no account, no queue. Ordering happens in a WhatsApp chat with the farm.

  1. Tap to chat

    Use any order button or the floating WhatsApp icon on this page.

  2. Tell us what you need

    Broilers, layers, or a crate of eggs — and how many. Mention if you want the bird dressed.

  3. We confirm price & stock

    Feed and market costs move, so we confirm today's price and availability before you pay.

  4. Delivery or pickup

    We arrange delivery within Lagos depending on order size. Pickup at the farm is also available.
