About the guide

Made for people who go.

Moonlight Events organizes festivals around the world in collaboration with municipalities and local city associations. We have been hosting festivals for many years, strengthening young people's understanding of other cultures.

Our main goal is to spread information about local festivals, discover talents, and support creativity. We encourage academic, professional, and cultural exchange between groups worldwide.

Questions

Before you plan

What is Moonlight Events?

Moonlight Events is a global festival organizer that promotes cultural exchange and creativity through festivals worldwide. This guide is our public calendar — a curated record of the celebrations we work with and the ones we travel to.

How can I attend a festival?

Open any festival in the calendar and save it to your list, then contact us via the Contact section. We will point you to the dates, the host city and practical details about attending.

Can I download festival information?

The guide is fully online, but every festival has a complete briefing — description, history, food and field notes. Save the ones you like and use your browser's print option to keep a copy.
