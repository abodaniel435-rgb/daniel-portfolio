01 SELECTED WORK

Built to be useful.

Twenty-six products and experiences made to solve practical problems — a paid real-estate client build, ten live sites you can open right now, and ten tools that do the maths behind everyday African economic problems, from payroll and school fees to fuel, harvest loss and import costs.

SHOW

i Every card opens real, working software. The six rebuilt sites and the ten new tools are published from this repository under demos/, so they load without a server and keep your data in your own browser — including the new ThatGuys Fitness coach studio. Each of the ten tools is a four-page site: the calculator itself, a data page carrying the published figures it works from, a playbook page with the step-by-step operating guide, and a record page that mirrors everything you saved. Hotel Miramar, Campus Coin, RentJury, CurrenSee and City of Gold also have their own live URLs. The full-stack PHP builds are packaged and deploy-ready; open a case study for the problem, the stack and the source.
